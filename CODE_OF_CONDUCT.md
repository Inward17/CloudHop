# Code of conduct

Cloudhop is built in the open, and everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and any
other project space) is expected to make it a respectful place to work.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to the tools.
- Disagree about ideas and code, not about people. Explain your reasoning and assume good intent.
- Give feedback that is specific and actionable, and accept feedback the same way.
- Respect people's time: keep threads on topic and say clearly what you need.
- Protect others' privacy and security. Never post someone's personal details, credentials or private messages.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or deliberate intimidation, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, or unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or repeatedly derailing a conversation.
- Publishing others' private information, or vulnerability details outside the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Encouraging any of the above.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this code, report it privately to the maintainers. Use GitHub's
"Report content" option on the comment or issue, or ask the maintainer (Inward17 on GitHub) for a private channel
first, as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not post the details in a public thread.

Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers will review them, may ask for more information, and will tell the
reporter what was done where that is appropriate.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code,
and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful to the community. The response
depends on the severity and on whether the behaviour continues after a warning.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

This code is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
