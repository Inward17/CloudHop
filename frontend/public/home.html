What Cloudhop does

One agent for everything between your repo and a running app: deploying it, moving it, watching it, and stopping what you no longer pay for.

Deploy to your cloud

Cloudhop reads your repo and opens a pull request with the pipeline, Compose file and server setup. Merge it, approve, and your app runs on AWS, GCP, DigitalOcean, Hetzner or your own server.

Start a deploy →

Move between providers

Switch clouds with the database copied and the old server kept as standby. Move sites between Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, Render, Koyeb, Northflank, Railway, Heroku, Fly.io, DigitalOcean App Platform, GitHub Pages and your own Coolify, settings and secrets included.

Plan a move →

Watch it, roll it back

Uptime and response checks every five minutes, spend and server load from your cloud, and a log of every action. Something wrong after a move? Roll back with one approval.

Stop paying twice

Pause old sites where the platform allows it, and remove standby servers when you are done. Cloudhop never deletes anything without asking.

Approve. Keep. Own. The three rules behind every action.

▪▪

Approve first

Every deploy, move and removal waits for a person. Removing a server asks you to type its name.

▪▪

Keep keys out

Clouds trust your CI with short-lived tokens. Where a cloud cannot, the token lives in your CI secrets.

▪▪

Own it all

Servers, data and pipeline files are yours, in your accounts and your repo. Leave whenever you like.

The agent only proposes. Fixed actions, fixed clouds, checked again in your pipeline.
